The Mark

Femme Fatale Creative — designed by instinct

Entesar's visual world begins in the dark. Not as emptiness — but as a space for focus, mystery, and instinct.

Inspired by the quiet power of black cats and the confidence of femme-fatale presence, the brand becomes a visual identity with a sharp gaze and soft control. It does not shout. It watches, understands, and strikes with intention.
